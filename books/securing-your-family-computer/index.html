About the guide

A cybersecurity guide written for real families.

Securing Your Family Computer explains a layered approach to home computer safety. It covers account hygiene, identity protection, Windows Security, ransomware controls, backups, browser and DNS guardrails, Microsoft Family Safety, and a monthly maintenance routine.

The guide is intentionally practical: each phase explains what to check, why it matters, and how families can build safer habits one step at a time.

Why it belongs in the portfolio

Clear technical communication is a cybersecurity skill.

This book demonstrates my ability to turn cybersecurity concepts into practical, user-focused guidance for nontechnical audiences — a key skill for IT support, security awareness, GRC, documentation, and client-facing technical work.

Inside the guide

The 5-phase family computer safety plan.

The guide organizes home cybersecurity into layers families can understand and maintain.

Phase 1

Account Hygiene

Updates, separate administrator accounts, standard daily users, child accounts, and privacy review.

Phase 2

Digital Identity

Password managers, MFA, backup codes, recovery planning, and connected-app cleanup.

Phase 3

Device Defense

Microsoft Defender, ransomware controls, backup layers, File History, and restore testing.

Phase 4–5

Guardrails & Routine

Ad blocking, DNS filtering, SafeSearch, Family Safety, monthly maintenance, and a family cyber agreement.
