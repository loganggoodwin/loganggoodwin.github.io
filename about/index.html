Logan Goodwin is an author, songwriter, technologist, humorist, and independent commentator based in New Hampshire. This page connects the creative work, technical background, and the life behind both.

About Logan

A creator with one foot in storytelling and the other in systems.

I write stories, build fictional worlds, create songs, study cybersecurity, design technical projects, and speak plainly about current events. Some of the work is serious. Some is deliberately ridiculous. Most of it begins with the same question: what happens when an ordinary person looks at something from a different angle?

My technical background gives the creative work structure and credibility. The creative work gives the technical side a human voice. Rather than splitting those identities into unrelated brands, this site presents them as parts of the same person.

Humor

Humor makes serious ideas easier to approach and ordinary frustrations worth remembering. Sometimes the joke is the best way into the conversation.

Education

Cybersecurity now. Technology leadership next.

I am finishing my master’s degree in cybersecurity and preparing to move into a PhD program in information technology management. My academic interests include cybersecurity, infrastructure, governance, systems thinking, technology leadership, and the relationship between people and complex technical systems.
