My story

Built a little different—and finally making that the brand.

My name is Logan Goodwin. I am an author, songwriter, cybersecurity professional, independent commentator, husband, father, grandfather, and someone who has spent most of his life learning how things work—and then wondering how they might work differently.

I write science fiction and satire. I make original music that can be sincere, ridiculous, or both. I study cybersecurity and build technical projects. I talk about current events, culture, technology, and public behavior in plain language. Those interests may look unrelated from a distance, but they all come from the same place: curiosity, storytelling, problem-solving, and an independent point of view.

I believe humor can carry a serious idea farther than outrage. I believe technology should be understandable. I believe a story should earn its reader's attention. And I believe civil disagreement still matters, especially when the subject is difficult.

I do not see books, music, technology, and commentary as separate identities. They are different tools for expressing the same voice.
