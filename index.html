About Logan

Author. Songwriter. Technologist. Husband, father, grandfather, and lifelong builder.

I am based in Somersworth, New Hampshire. My work crosses fiction, music, cybersecurity, humor, and commentary because I have never believed a person has to fit inside one category.

I like creating things that are memorable, useful, honest, or entertaining—preferably more than one at the same time.
