Featured message

Protest is a right. Violence is not a protest tactic.

The message behind “Put the Rock Down” is simple: people may protest, criticize, march, and disagree. Throwing rocks, using vehicles as weapons, attacking people, and destroying property cross a line into violence.

Editorial standard

Independent does not mean careless.

Commentary should distinguish facts, opinions, questions, jokes, and conclusions. I may be blunt, but the goal is to make an argument people can understand and respond to—not merely to generate outrage.

Agree, disagree, laugh, challenge the premise—but keep the conversation human.
