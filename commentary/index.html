Independent commentary

Disagreement does not require dehumanization.

My commentary begins with a simple standard: people have the right to disagree, criticize leaders, and protest. Violence, attacking people, throwing rocks, and using vehicles as weapons are not peaceful protest. That distinction should not depend on which political side is involved.

Current events

What happened—and what is being ignored?

Commentary focused on conduct, incentives, contradictions, and the parts of a story that disappear inside partisan framing.

Technology

Platforms, AI & digital power

Questions about discovery, moderation, privacy, security, artificial intelligence, and the systems that shape what people see.

Culture

Adults should be able to talk.

A preference for direct conversation, consistent standards, and less performative outrage from every direction.

Humor

Because the world needs the joke.

Sarcasm, parody, absurd premises, self-deprecation, and jokes about the everyday problems nobody requested.

Editorial standard

The rule is consistency.

If conduct is wrong when the opposing side does it, it should remain wrong when your side does it. Commentary is strongest when it applies the same standard to allies, opponents, institutions, and the commentator himself.
